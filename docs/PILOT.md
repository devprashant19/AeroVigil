## 2. Summary Results

* **Total Participants:** 48 community members in Delhi-NCR.
* **Shifted Schedule:** **39 of 48 (81.3%)** reported shifting their outdoor exercise, walking, or ventilation schedule to the afternoon cleanest window advised by AeroVigil.
* **Did Not Shift:** **9 of 48 (18.8%)** could not shift due to immutable external constraints (fixed school start hours, frontline work shifts, public transit timetables, pet routines, or temple pujas).
* **Severe Smog Days:** On days when AeroVigil issued a *"Stay Indoors Enforced"* verdict (no daylight hour below safe thresholds), participants reported remaining indoors rather than exercising outdoors.

---

## 3. What this is not (honest methodology and limits)

* **Not a clinical trial:** There was no medical oversight, no control group, no spirometer, and no biomarker testing.
* **Self-reported by message:** Participants' usage and schedule adherence were reported informally by WhatsApp text messages and voice notes, not tracked by telemetry or in-app location logging.
* **No clinical health claims:** While several participants colloquially noted reduced coughing or throat burning compared to previous morning walks, these are subjective personal anecdotes. We make zero clinical health claims.
* **Retrospective backtest separation:** The **42.0% PM2.5 inhalation reduction** figure is a mathematical outcome derived from 13 government CAAQMS monitoring station-days in `docs/BACKTEST_OUTCOMES.md`; it represents what the advisory mathematically yields across ground monitors, distinct from participant self-reports.
